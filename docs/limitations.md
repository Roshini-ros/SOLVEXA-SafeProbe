# Limitations and responsible use

- The signal model is synthetic and does not reproduce every motor design, operating point, or fault interaction.
- Fault effects may be nonlinear and correlated.
- A fault may not be identifiable from the available sensors.
- Safety is represented only by a prototype-level exclusion rule; this is not a real-world risk assessment.
- The hidden scenario selector is for repeatable developer evaluation and should not be presented as an operator-facing diagnostic feature.
- Never use these results as instructions for real equipment maintenance or operation.
